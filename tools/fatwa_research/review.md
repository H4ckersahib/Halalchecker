# Fatwa-Quellen (nur askimam)

Stand: aktuelle Regeln in `tools/halal_rulings.json`. Alle Links führen direkt zu askimam.org.

## Entscheidungen des Projekts

- en:e904 shellac: HARAM in every school (owner, after askimam)
- en:e120 carmine: HARAM in every school (owner)
- en:e1510 ethanol: MASHBOOH, doubt alcohol (owner, after askimam: forbidden from grapes or dates, permissible from other sources if not intoxicating)
- en:alcohol (the ingredient word Alkohol): MASHBOOH like ethanol (owner); every alcoholic drink has its own HARAM ruling
